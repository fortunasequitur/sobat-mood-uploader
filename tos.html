Sobat Mood Uploader ("the App") is a private, single-user automation tool. It is operated solely by the owner of the Sobat Mood YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@sobatmood) to upload the owner's own finished videos to that same channel through the official YouTube Data API v3.

The App is not a public service. It has exactly one user: the channel owner. It is not distributed, licensed, or offered to any third party, and it never accesses any Google or YouTube account other than the owner's own.

3. Acceptable use

  • Use of the App complies with the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy: https://fortunasequitur.github.io/sobat-mood-uploader/privacy.html — in short, OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own server, and no data is shared with third parties.
