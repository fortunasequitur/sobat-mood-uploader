Sobat Mood Uploader

Sobat Mood Uploader is a private, single-purpose automation tool used by the owner of the Sobat Mood YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos through the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

The tool uploads finished video files (including YouTube Shorts) to the owner's own channel on a schedule, sets titles, descriptions and thumbnails from the owner's templates, and records each published video in a local log. It is operated solely by the channel owner for their own channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.
